Last updated: 19 September 2026

Short version. Sakugame is a free fan project with no ads and no trackers. You don't need an email address to play. What's stored is a username, a hashed password, an optional avatar, an optional AniList username and your game stats. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. You can delete your account at any time from the Account tab.

  • Running your account and the games (username, password, room data, stats) — performance of the service you ask for by creating an account.
  • AniList sync and profile picture — your consent. Both are optional and can be withdrawn at any time by clearing the AniList field or removing your picture.

  • Technical logs — legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working.

  • Google (Firebase), as a technical processor: database, authentication and real-time hosting.
  • GitHub, as the host of the site files.

No data is sold, rented or passed to advertisers, data brokers or ad networks. The site carries no advertising and no analytics.

✈️ Transfers outside the European Union

Google and GitHub are US companies and may process some data outside the EU. These transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, on those companies' certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

⏳ How long data is kept

  • Account (username, password, avatar, AniList link, stats): until you delete your account.
  • Inactive accounts: may be removed after 24 months without a login.
  • Rooms and chat messages: deleted when the game ends or shortly after the room closes.
  • Technical logs: kept by Firebase for a limited period, in the range of weeks to months.

🗑️ Deleting your account

Account → Danger zone → Delete my account. Deletion is permanent and removes the username, password, avatar, AniList link and stats.

What may remain afterwards: messages already sent in an ongoing game, and Firebase technical logs until they expire. Write to me if you want those removed sooner.

🧾 Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time.

To use these rights, write to [email protected] from the username concerned, or message me on Discord. I usually reply within a few days. Since accounts have no email attached, you may be asked to show you control the account, for example by logging into it while we talk.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with your own national authority.

🍪 Cookies & local storage

Sakugame uses no advertising cookies and no analytics. It only uses your browser's local storage and Firebase's technical cookies, to keep you logged in and remember your preferences: language, 18+ filter, saved game configurations. These are strictly necessary to run the service, so no consent banner is required. Clearing your browser data removes them.

🔒 Security

Passwords are stored hashed. Traffic runs over HTTPS and data access is restricted by Firebase security rules. No online service is bulletproof, so use a password you don't use anywhere else. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, you will be told and the supervisory authority will be notified.

👶 Minors

Sakugame is open from age 13. In France and the EU, users under 15 need permission from a parent or guardian to create an account.

The site includes player chat and a filter that hides 18+ content, available in the Account tab. A parent or guardian can ask for a minor's account to be deleted by writing to the contact address.
