Anime Guess Who?

Classic guessing game with your favorite anime characters from AniList. Eliminate characters and guess your opponent's secret!

Undercover

3-8 players! Everyone secretly gets an anime word — except the Undercover (a similar word) and Mr. White (no word at all). Give a clue each round, discuss, and vote out the impostor!

🎭

Guess Who — Battle Royale

3-8 players! Everyone has a secret character. Ask questions to the whole table, track each opponent with their own ❌ color, guess their secrets, and climb the points ranking!

⚡

Guess Who — Race

3-8 players! One player is the TARGET with a mystery character. Hunters take turns asking questions — the first to find the mystery character wins!
