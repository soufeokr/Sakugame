Available Games

Anime Guess Who?

Classic guessing game with your favorite anime characters from AniList. Eliminate characters and guess your opponent's secret!

Undercover

3-8 players! Everyone secretly gets an anime word — except the Undercover (a similar word) and Mr. White (no word at all). Give a clue each round, discuss, and vote out the impostor!

Guess Who — Battle Royale

3-8 players! Everyone has a secret character. Ask questions to the whole table, track each opponent on their own glowing board, guess their secrets, and climb the points ranking!

Guess Who — Race

3-8 players! One player is the TARGET with a mystery character. Hunters take turns ASKING questions — but everyone can GUESS at any moment (wrong guess = -1 life)! First to find the mystery character wins!

Blur Guess

Solo or up to 8 players! A blurred character or anime cover slowly clears over 5 stages — name it as early as you can for big points. The fastest correct guesses score a speed bonus!

Guess Who — Hot & Cold

2-6 players! One hides a character, everyone else proposes characters at their own pace and the hider scores each 0-100. Every guess feeds your cumulative total — biggest total wins!
